## Note on university buildings

These are public photographs of historic university architecture in Padua,
Bologna and Venice. They illustrate the setting of higher education in Italy and
do **not** imply any partnership, affiliation or endorsement by the institutions
shown. Do not add captions or claims that would suggest otherwise.

## Optimisation

The files are unoptimised originals (~5 MB total). Before launch, run them
through an image optimiser (Squoosh, ImageOptim, `sharp`) and consider exporting
WebP/AVIF versions — this is the single biggest performance win available on
this page.
